再读一遍：苏文查资料

Su Wen is a student at our school radio station. Every week, she looks through a children's encyclopaedia to find new stories. When she does not know a word, she looks it up in a dictionary. When she loses her notebook, she looks for it everywhere. She also looks after her little brother at home and looks at the pictures in the book with him. Last week, she read about dinosaurs. Dinosaurs died out about 66 million years ago, but they were not all the same. Some were as small as chickens. Others were as big as ten elephants. Su Wen thinks Leonardo da Vinci was very intelligent. He was also talented in many different fields: painting, music, science and more. She wants to be an intelligent student too, so she looks through her books every day.

分清楚：look up 查阅（查词）— look for 寻找（找东西）；look after 照顾（照顾人）— look at 看（看东西）。
